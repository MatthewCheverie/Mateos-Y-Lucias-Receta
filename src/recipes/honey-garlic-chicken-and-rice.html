honey garlic chicken

Description

A delicious honey garlic chicken recipe

Ingredients

  • panko
  • corn starch
  • choice of oil to fry
  • chicken thigh
  • eggs
  • pepper
  • salt
  • parsley
  • garlic powder
  • onion powder
  • honey
  • soy suace
  • sesame oil
  • minced galric
  • optional cook white rice to serve along with

Steps

  1. Prep: put panko and corn starch in a bowl along with all the seasonings, chop chicken thighs into desired sizes, heat oil, whisk egg into a bowl
  2. Coat the chicken in cornstarch, then the eggs wash and flour before putting into hot oil to fry
  3. Cook all pieces until golden browned and cooked
  4. Suace prep: In a hot skillet combine garlic, honey, soy suace, sesame oil and corn starch slurry
  5. Once suace is thickened readd the chciken and toss until coats the chicken
  6. Can be served on a plate with some white rice
